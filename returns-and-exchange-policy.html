Return, Exchange & Shipping Policy

Thank you for shopping at Abaya Majesty.

Returns & Exchanges

Due to the nature of our products - and to maintain hygiene and modesty standards - we do not offer refunds or exchanges. All sales are final.

Order Cancellations

Once an order is placed, it cannot be canceled or modified. Please review your order carefully before confirming your purchase.

Shipping Policy

We offer reasonable shipping charges depending on your region. We use DHL, FedEx, and local express services based on availability and delivery times.

  • Orders are typically dispatched within 15–18 days.
  • Pre-ordered products may take 20–25 working days to dispatch.
  • Couriers do not operate on weekends and bank holidays.

Once your order is packed and picked up, you will receive an email with your tracking number. To check the status, click the tracking link in that email. If you didn’t receive a tracking number, please contact us at abayamajesty0312@gmail.com.
